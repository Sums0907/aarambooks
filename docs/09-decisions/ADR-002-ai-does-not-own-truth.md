# ADR-002: AI Does Not Own Truth

## Status
Accepted

## Decision

Artificial intelligence capabilities within AaramBooks do not own business truth.

AI systems may interpret information, identify patterns, provide reasoning, and support decisions, but final ownership of operational truth remains with the responsible business systems.

## Architectural Intent

This prevents intelligence systems from becoming hidden sources of operational authority.

Trust is maintained by ensuring that:
- Business facts originate from business domains.
- Intelligence is derived from trusted facts.
- Governance controls how intelligence influences future actions.

## Governance Principle

AI enhances understanding.

AI does not replace business ownership.

Business systems create truth.

Aaram Brain creates intelligence from that truth.
