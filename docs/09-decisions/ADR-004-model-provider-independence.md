# ADR-004: Model Provider Independence

## Status
Accepted

## Decision

AaramBooks architecture preserves independence between Aaram Brain capabilities and external AI model providers.

Intelligence architecture must not depend on a single external intelligence provider as the owner of reasoning capability.

## Architectural Intent

This decision protects long-term evolution by ensuring:
- Intelligence capabilities remain Aaram-owned.
- External providers remain capability providers.
- Provider changes do not redefine ecosystem architecture.

## Governance Principle

External intelligence providers support Aaram Brain.

They do not own Aaram Brain.

AaramBooks controls intelligence intent, governance, and evolution.
