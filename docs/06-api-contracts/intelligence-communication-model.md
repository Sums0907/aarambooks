# Intelligence Communication Model

## Purpose

This model defines how intelligence capabilities communicate across AaramBooks.

## Communication Layers

### Business Systems to Aaram Brain

Business systems provide trusted operational information.

Aaram Brain interprets and reasons over this information.

### Brain Core to Intelligence Domains

Brain Core provides reusable intelligence capabilities.

Intelligence domains apply these capabilities to specific business objectives.

### Intelligence Domains to Intelligence Applications

Intelligence domains provide intelligence outcomes for business experiences.

## Boundary Principle

Communication enables collaboration.

Communication does not merge ownership.
