# Contract Ownership Principles

## Purpose

Contract ownership ensures every communication boundary has clear responsibility.

## Producer Responsibility

A producer owns:

- The meaning of information it exposes.
- The correctness of its domain information.
- The stability of the capability it provides.

## Consumer Responsibility

A consumer owns:

- How consumed information is interpreted.
- How intelligence or experiences are created from it.
- Its own internal decisions.

## Ownership Rule

A contract consumer never becomes the owner of producer truth.

Aaram Brain consuming business information does not make Brain the business authority.

## Principle

Ownership follows responsibility, not accessibility.
