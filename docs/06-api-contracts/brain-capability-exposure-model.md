# Brain Capability Exposure Model

## Purpose

Aaram Brain exposes intelligence capabilities without exposing internal ownership or operational responsibilities.

## Capability Categories

Conceptually, Brain capabilities include:

- Understanding business context.
- Reasoning over information.
- Supporting decisions.
- Generating recommendations.
- Enabling intelligent actions.

## Exposure Principle

Capabilities are shared as intelligence services, not as replacement business systems.

## Boundary

Aaram Brain provides intelligence.

Business systems remain responsible for execution truth.
