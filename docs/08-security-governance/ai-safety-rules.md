# AI Safety Rules

## Purpose

Define responsible intelligence boundaries for Aaram Brain.

## Principles

Aaram Brain creates intelligence from trusted business truth. It does not create or replace operational truth.

AI decisions must remain:

- Explainable.
- Governed.
- Reviewable.
- Aligned with business responsibility.

## Intelligence Boundaries

Business systems own business facts.

Aaram Brain may:

- Interpret information.
- Identify patterns.
- Recommend actions.

Aaram Brain must not:

- Modify truth ownership.
- Override domain authority.
- Create duplicate operational records.

## Responsible AI Evolution

Future intelligence capabilities must preserve trust, accountability, and human oversight.
