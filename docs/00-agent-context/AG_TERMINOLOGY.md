# AG Terminology

## AaramBooks

The complete Aaram ecosystem.

## Business Domain

A business capability area with ownership responsibility.

## Business System

A deterministic system that maintains operational truth.

## Aaram Brain

The intelligence and decision layer of AaramBooks.

## Brain Core

Reusable intelligence foundation shared by Intelligence Domains.

## Intelligence Domain

A specialized intelligence capability focused on a business objective.

Initial domains:
- NDR Intelligence
- Customer Query Intelligence

## Truth Layer

Systems responsible for authoritative operational information.

## Intelligence Layer

Systems responsible for understanding, reasoning, recommendations and automation.

## API Contract

A controlled interface defining communication between systems.

## Event

A business occurrence communicated between systems through defined event mechanisms.
