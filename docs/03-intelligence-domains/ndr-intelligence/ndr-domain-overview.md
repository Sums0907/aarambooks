# NDR Intelligence Domain Overview

## Purpose

NDR Intelligence applies Aaram Brain Core capabilities to reduce delivery failures through intelligent understanding, recommendations, and resolution support.

## Ownership Boundary

Business systems remain the source of truth for orders, shipments, delivery events, customer information, and operational outcomes.

Aaram Brain transforms business truth into intelligence. NDR Intelligence applies that intelligence to support decisions and improve delivery outcomes.

## Responsibilities

- Understand delivery failure patterns.
- Identify resolution opportunities.
- Provide decision support for operational teams.
- Learn from historical outcomes.

## Non-Responsibilities

NDR Intelligence does not create operational truth, replace business systems, or own delivery execution.
