# What is Non-Deliverd Report (NDR)?

A Non-Delivery Report, also known as an NDR, is a report that provides information about orders that were not delivered in a delivery attempt, and the reason for their non-delivery. Essentially, NDR acts as a receipt or confirmation that a delivery attempt was made but was unsuccessful. It can be used to identify issues that prevented delivery, such as an incorrect shipping address or an unavailable recipient, and help sellers take corrective action to resolve the issue.

### Take Action

This tab addresses recent NDR (non-delivery) orders by identifying the cause, resolving the issue, and re-attempting delivery. Required actions depend on the reason for the non-delivery. Promptly addressing NDR orders enhances delivery percentage and customer satisfaction.

### Re-Attempt Requested

It refers to the process of attempting delivery for orders that have been marked as non-delivered (NDR). This can occur when the initial delivery attempt was not successful and additional steps are taken to ensure that the order can be successfully delivered. It could be multiple attempts made to deliver the same order.

### Delivered

This tab shows an order that was initially marked as NDR (not delivered) due to the inability to deliver the package to the customer’s doorstep, but the issue was later resolved and the order was successfully delivered to the customer. This can happen if the customer provides a new delivery address or is available for receiving the order when it is reattempted.

### RTO Requested

RTO (return to origin) Requested refers to a status update for an order that was earlier marked as NDR (not delivered), indicating that the package has been requested to be returned to the origin or sender’s location. This can happen when the delivery attempt is unsuccessful, and the customer is unable to receive the package, or when the customer refuses to accept the delivery. The RTO request is made to initiate the process of returning the package to the sender’s location.
