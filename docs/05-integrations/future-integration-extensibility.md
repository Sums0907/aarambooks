# Future Integration Extensibility Principles

## Purpose

AaramBooks Integration Architecture must support expansion of future business systems, intelligence domains, and applications.

## Extensibility Principles

### Domain Independence

New business domains should connect without redesigning the Brain foundation.

### Capability Reuse

Shared intelligence capabilities should be reusable across domains.

### Boundary Preservation

Expansion should not weaken ownership separation.

### Progressive Evolution

Integration maturity can grow while preserving architectural foundations.

## Long-Term Vision

Aaram Brain becomes an intelligence platform connected to an expanding ecosystem of business truths and intelligence applications.

The foundation remains:

Business systems create truth.
Aaram Brain creates intelligence from that truth.
