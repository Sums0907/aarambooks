# Brain Information Consumption Model

## Purpose

Aaram Brain consumes trusted information from business systems to create intelligence.

The Brain does not independently establish business truth.

## Information Flow

Business Systems
↓
Trusted Information
↓
Context Understanding
↓
Reasoning
↓
Decision Intelligence
↓
Actions or Recommendations

## Trusted Information

Information consumed by Aaram Brain should represent meaningful business reality created by responsible systems.

## Context Before Intelligence

The Brain requires understanding of:

- Business situation.
- Customer context.
- Historical interactions.
- Operational conditions.
- Relevant knowledge.

## Separation

Operational state belongs to business systems.

Interpretation, reasoning, and intelligence belong to Aaram Brain.
