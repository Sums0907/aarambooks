# Integration Ownership Principles

## Ownership Foundation

Every integration must preserve the rule:

Business systems own operational truth.
Aaram Brain owns intelligence creation.

## Business System Responsibilities

Business systems remain responsible for:

- Creating operational records.
- Maintaining transactional truth.
- Managing business workflows.
- Enforcing operational rules.

## Aaram Brain Responsibilities

Aaram Brain is responsible for:

- Understanding received context.
- Building intelligence.
- Applying reasoning.
- Creating decisions and recommendations.
- Supporting intelligence applications.

## Ownership Boundaries

Integration must never:

- Move operational ownership into Aaram Brain.
- Create parallel business records.
- Replace domain systems.
- Become a hidden operational workflow engine.

## Principle

Integration connects intelligence to truth without changing who owns truth.
