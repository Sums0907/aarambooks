# Intelligence Output Model

## Purpose

Aaram Brain provides intelligence outputs to intelligence applications and business processes without owning their execution environment.

## Output Categories

Aaram Brain may provide:

- Understanding.
- Recommendations.
- Decisions.
- Predictions.
- Prioritization.
- Guidance.

## Application Responsibility

Intelligence applications use Brain capabilities to solve domain problems.

Examples include:

- NDR intelligence.
- Customer query intelligence.
- Future intelligence domains.

## Boundary

Aaram Brain creates intelligence.

Applications decide how intelligence is presented or applied within their responsibilities.

## Principle

The Brain provides intelligence capability, not operational replacement.
