Brand Overview:
Aaram Homes is a premium home-linen, bedding, and home decor brand operating primarily online in India. The brand specializes in high-quality comfort products for the bedroom and elegant decor for the entire home.

Product Terminology:
- Dohar: A traditional Indian summer blanket. It typically consists of three layers, making it lightweight and suitable for mild temperatures or summer nights.
- Fitted Sheet: A bedsheet designed with elasticated corners to securely grip the mattress and prevent slipping.
- Flat Sheet: A traditional rectangular bedsheet without elasticated corners.
