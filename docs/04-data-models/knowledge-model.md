# Knowledge Model

## Purpose

The Knowledge Model defines how Aaram Brain understands and organizes knowledge.

Knowledge represents understood information derived from trusted sources.

## Knowledge Categories

Conceptually, knowledge includes:

- Business knowledge.
- Domain knowledge.
- Customer understanding.
- Operational understanding.
- Historical learning.
- Decision knowledge.

## Ownership

Business systems own operational truth.

Aaram Brain owns intelligence interpretation and knowledge organization.

Aaram Brain must not replace source systems as the owner of facts.

## Relationship

Business Truth → Knowledge Understanding → Intelligence Capability
