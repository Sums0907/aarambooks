# Customer Context Model

## Purpose

Defines the conceptual representation of customer-related intelligence context.

## Principle

Customer context is not a replacement customer database.

It is an intelligence view created from trusted business information.

## Context Includes

- Customer interactions.
- Customer needs.
- Previous conversations.
- Resolution history.
- Relevant business situations.

## Ownership Boundary

Customer master truth belongs to business systems.

Customer intelligence context belongs to Aaram Brain.
