# Implementation Ownership Principles

## Purpose

Implementation planning must preserve ownership boundaries established by AaramBooks architecture.

## Business Systems Ownership

Business systems remain responsible for:

- Operational truth.
- Domain responsibility.
- Business processes.
- Source-of-truth decisions.

Implementation planning must not transfer business ownership into Aaram Brain.

## Aaram Brain Ownership

Aaram Brain remains responsible for:

- Intelligence creation.
- Context understanding.
- Reasoning support.
- Decision assistance.
- Responsible intelligence evolution.

Aaram Brain consumes trusted truth; it does not become the owner of operational truth.

## Intelligence Domain Ownership

Intelligence domains evolve as governed capability areas.

Planning must ensure:

- Clear capability boundaries.
- Controlled evolution.
- Alignment with business value.
- Preservation of governance requirements.

## Governance Ownership

Security and governance remain continuous responsibilities throughout evolution.

No implementation direction should bypass:

- Safety principles.
- Audit expectations.
- Privacy requirements.
- Permission boundaries.
