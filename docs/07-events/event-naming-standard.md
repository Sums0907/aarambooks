# Event Naming Standard

## Purpose

Event naming should preserve business meaning and make ownership boundaries understandable.

## Principles

Event names should represent:

- Meaningful business occurrences.
- Domain ownership.
- Business facts.

Event names should not represent:

- Technical operations.
- Infrastructure actions.
- Internal implementation details.

## Ownership Clarity

The originating business domain must remain clear.

Events describe what happened within an owned business capability.

## Evolution

Naming conventions must preserve long-term business understanding as the ecosystem grows.
