# Inbound Events

## Definition

Inbound events represent business changes consumed by Aaram Brain or intelligence capabilities.

## Role

Inbound events allow Aaram Brain to understand trusted changes from business systems.

## Consumption Principles

Aaram Brain:

- Consumes trusted business information.
- Builds context.
- Enables reasoning.
- Creates intelligence.

Aaram Brain does not become the owner of inbound business facts.

## Boundary

Inbound events provide awareness, not operational control.
