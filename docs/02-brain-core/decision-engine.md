# Decision Engine

## 1. Purpose

The Decision Engine is responsible for supporting intelligent
decision-making across AaramBooks.

It evaluates situations, available knowledge, and reasoning outputs to
provide recommendations.

It does not own business decisions.

The foundational rule is:

> Business systems create truth. Aaram Brain creates intelligence from
> that truth.

## 2. Responsibility

The Decision Engine provides:

-   Recommendation support.
-   Decision analysis.
-   Alternative evaluation.
-   Outcome consideration.

## 3. Boundaries

The Decision Engine does not:

-   Override business authorities.
-   Change operational truth.
-   Replace human approval where required.

## 4. Final Statement

The Decision Engine enables better decisions by transforming
intelligence into actionable recommendations.
