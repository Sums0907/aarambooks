# Reasoning Engine

## 1. Purpose

The Reasoning Engine is a core capability of Aaram Brain Core
responsible for analysing trusted business information and generating
understanding from relationships, patterns, and situations.

It does not create business truth.

It reasons over truth created by business systems.

The foundational rule is:

> Business systems create truth. Aaram Brain creates intelligence from
> that truth.

## 2. Responsibility

The Reasoning Engine provides:

-   Situation analysis.
-   Pattern understanding.
-   Relationship interpretation.
-   Scenario evaluation.
-   Intelligence generation support.

## 3. Boundaries

The Reasoning Engine does not:

-   Own operational decisions.
-   Modify business records.
-   Replace business rules.
-   Become a business authority.

It supports intelligence; business systems retain ownership.

## 4. Relationship

Context provides the situation.

Knowledge provides meaning.

Reasoning provides interpretation.

Decision provides recommendations.

Action provides controlled enablement.

## 5. Final Statement

The Reasoning Engine transforms understood business information into
intelligence insights while preserving domain ownership.
