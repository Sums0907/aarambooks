# Knowledge Engine

## 1. Purpose

The Knowledge Engine is a core capability of Aaram Brain Core responsible for maintaining and providing ecosystem understanding.

Its purpose is to enable Aaram Brain to understand business concepts, rules, relationships, processes, and accumulated knowledge required for intelligent reasoning.

The Knowledge Engine does not create business truth.

It organizes and interprets knowledge derived from trusted sources.

The foundational rule is:

> Business systems create truth. Aaram Brain creates intelligence from that truth.

---

The Knowledge Engine provides the understanding foundation required by reasoning, decision, and action capabilities.

# 3. Core Responsibility

The Knowledge Engine is responsible for managing reusable ecosystem knowledge.

Its responsibilities include:

- Understanding business concepts.
- Maintaining relationships between concepts.
- Preserving organizational knowledge.
- Supporting intelligence interpretation.
- Providing relevant knowledge for reasoning.

---

# 4. Knowledge Categories

## 4.1 Business Knowledge

Represents understanding of business operations.

Examples:

- Business processes.
- Operational concepts.
- Domain terminology.
- Business rules.

Business knowledge explains how AaramBooks operates.

---

## 4.2 Domain Knowledge

Represents knowledge specific to business domains.

Examples:

### Inventory Domain

- Product concepts.
- Stock concepts.
- Inventory lifecycle understanding.

### Packing Domain

- Warehouse execution concepts.
- Packing lifecycle understanding.

### Customer Domain

- Customer interaction concepts.
- Service understanding.

Domain knowledge improves intelligence without taking ownership of domain truth.

---

## 4.3 Organizational Knowledge

Represents knowledge about how Aaram operates.

Examples:

- Policies.
- Procedures.
- Standards.
- Architectural principles.

---

## 4.4 Historical Knowledge

Represents accumulated understanding from previous situations.

Examples:

- Previous outcomes.
- Past decisions.
- Repeated patterns.

Historical knowledge supports learning and improvement.

---

# 7. Knowledge Boundaries

The Knowledge Engine must not:

- Become a business database.
- Replace operational systems.
- Modify business records.
- Own domain transactions.
- Override business rules.

Knowledge explains truth.

It does not create truth.
